# Service Terms Baseline

These are implementation requirements for operator terms, not legal advice or
a ready-to-publish contract. Before commercial launch, counsel must identify the
operator/customer, service scope, authorized users, subscription/payment terms,
acceptable use, tenant administrator responsibilities, Microsoft/Stripe terms,
data protection roles, confidentiality, intellectual property, support/SLA,
warranties, liability, indemnity, suspension/termination, export/deletion,
governing law, notices, and change procedure.

Terms must state that Stripe billing is an Application Owner control-plane
function, tenants are responsible for authority to connect Microsoft 365 and
process directory/signature data, and third-party service availability is not
controlled by Signify. No healthcare claim may be made until the PHI assessment,
vendor agreements, operational controls, and required business associate
agreements are complete.
