# Privacy Notice Baseline

This file is the product-data baseline an operator must adapt with legal counsel
and publish with its legal entity, contact details, jurisdictions, effective
date, and customer agreement before accepting production users.

Signify processes account identity, organization membership, signature/profile
content, Microsoft tenant directory data authorized by the tenant, campaign and
template data, service telemetry, audit history, and Application Owner billing
metadata. It uses that data to authenticate users, provide signature workflows,
operate integrations, enforce subscriptions, secure and support the service,
recover customer data, and meet legal obligations.

Data is shared only with contracted infrastructure and integration providers
needed to deliver the service, the tenant that controls the workspace, and
authorities when legally required. The current provider categories are listed
in `docs/SUBPROCESSORS.md`. Signify does not sell tenant content.

Users submit access, correction, export, deletion, or objection requests to the
operator's published privacy contact. The operator verifies authority and
coordinates tenant-controlled requests with the relevant tenant administrator.
Retention follows `docs/DATA-RETENTION.md`. Cross-border transfer terms,
cookies, lawful bases, children's-data policy, and regulator contact details
must be completed for the operator's actual market before launch.
